01The problem ~1.5 min

We can't see what we're losing
fast enough to save it.

A problem cooperative formation, regen practice, and place-based work all share.

ONE
Cooperatives form in places that weren't asking — and fold quietly when the imported model doesn't fit.
TWO
Stewards collapse before anyone notices the load they were carrying.
THREE
Connections fade in years that look like normal time. By the time we name the loss, the relationships are gone.

When the place can't see itself, it can't tend itself.

02Where most fail ~1.5 min

Three patterns we keep watching miss the mark.

Existing approaches see something. None see the cascade.

ONE
Inventory over flow. Directories count entities. They don't measure what moves between them, or where the energy stops moving.
TWO
Single-instance interpretation. A finding alone is opinion. Without comparison to comparable healthy places, it stays opinion.
THREE
Inspiration over diagnosis. Regen frameworks teach principles. They don't tell you which specific connection in your specific bioregion is approaching collapse.

All three miss because they don't read connections at scale, against reference.

03What we do differently ~2 min

We built diagnostic medicine for bioregions.

Read connections. Compare like-for-like. Catch stress before collapse.

ONE
Per-edge measurement. Every connection carries what flows, magnitude, direction, pressure on it, reciprocity. The mirror sees the cascade.
TWO
Composite reference ranges. "Healthy" is built from multiple comparable peer-bioregions, weighted per-lens. No single sister has to mirror whole.
THREE
Early warning before collapse. Variance and autocorrelation per edge over time. Multi-metric agreement before alarm. The signal arrives early enough to act.

When a measurement leaves the band, the methodology surfaces it before the place loses what it didn't know it had.

04What it produces ~2 min

Read → compare → match.

Measurement turns into pathway, not just findings.

PLACE
For the place: stress patterns surfaced, latent capacity named, opportunities ranked by leverage — all in the place's own decision-shape.
PEER
For practitioners: evidence-backed mentorship matches — people who walked similar threads in similar places, with documented outcomes.
FIELD
For the field: every closed pathway becomes expressed proof for the next bioregion. The library grows. Diagnostic power scales.

Read → compare → diagnose → opportunity → expressed proof → curated mentorship → connection restored.

Reading is the input. Connection restored is the output.
