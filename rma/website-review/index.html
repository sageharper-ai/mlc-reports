We're building a new website for the Regenerative Media Alliance, and a first draft is up at regenmedia.studio/mockup-v1. Before we build the real thing, we want to see it through the eyes of the people it's for.

Each review below puts you in someone else's shoes, like a newcomer, a guest or someone with a story to bring. You click through the pages that person would visit, say how each step went, answer a few questions and submit. One review takes about five minutes. One is helpful, three is great. The draft isn't finished, so some boxes say "pending" and the forms don't send anything yet. That's expected.
