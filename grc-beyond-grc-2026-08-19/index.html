2 · What GRC has actually tried (Slack sweep 2021 → today: team channels, transparency, newsletters, upcoming-meetings)

Before we wish for anything new, look at what has already been tried, because most of last week's wishes have a history. This morning I counted every non-archived channel in GRC's Slack through Slack's own API (members, last post, posts in 30 / 90 / 365 days), and read the big community channels, the team channels Sage is in, #grc-transparency back to 2021, and this lane's full 13 months. Numbers below are measured, not impressions; the message-reading was Sage's channels only. Each row: what exists → who is actually there → what it produces → so what. Two things jump out. The original promise of GRC was "a pre-incubator, a playground for experimenting," written down in 2021 and never given a structure. And the Governance group decided, the same hour we ran Part 1, to start cross-group meetings on exactly this question. Neither room knew. That's the ground we're standing on today.

Takeaways
  1. Registered ≠ active — measured, not guessed. ~1,400 people, ~50 channels, ~84 posts in 30 days, 13 channels with any post, 17 silent for a year. Seven live rooms carry the network. Design experiments for the people who show up.
  2. Live rooms work; Slack broadcasts but doesn't converse. #_general and #_promote are healthy bulletin boards; almost nothing threads or carries forward, in any lane.
  3. Governance has the process and no proposals; this room has the wants and no process. That's the seam — we are the intake, they are the structure. Same hour, two rooms, neither knew.
  4. The 2021 "pre-incubator" promise is the experiment everyone keeps asking for. Nobody has to invent it; someone has to give it a shape and an owner.

4 · What can we try? (rebuilt after Aug 19 — for Aug 26)

We didn't reach this tab on Aug 19 and that was right — the room wanted to stay in 2 and 3. This tab is a seed bed, not a shortlist. It draws from three wells: A · what this room said, B · what GRC itself has already tried (five years of Slack — things that faded are candidates for a different version, not dead ends), and C · what neighboring regenerative networks do (sourced; links at the bottom). Use any of it to spark something not on the page. One rule holds across all three wells — David's: "you can't assign me your new idea" — a thing becomes an experiment when someone will carry it for 30 days. John Buck moves to Sep 2.

First, the frame: value is diverse — so the answer is a portfolio, not a winner

The poll was built with many kinds of value on purpose, and the spread proved the point: every single option got at least one vote. Guest interviews 8/9, Open Office 5/9, Governance 4/9, hot seat 3/9, Regen Ag 2/9, workshops 3/9, fourth-wall 2/9, other 2/9, book club 1/9. Different people come for different things and want to engage differently — and the ones who keep the machine going are not all the same type. So on Aug 26 we are not choosing the experiment. We are seeding several small ones, each aimed at a different attractor. The map below is a first cut from the room — argue with it.

Well B · From GRC's own history — tried once; what's the different version?

Everything here is in Slack. None of it "failed" — most simply lost its carrier. Re-read each as a prototype that ran once.

  • The 2021 promise: "GRC as pre-incubator — a playground for experimenting" (Sam Greenwood, #grc-transparency, Dec 2021). Never given a shape. Different version: a standing Prototype Desk — 15 minutes in this lane where anyone brings an earliest idea and gets three reactions and one offer; logged in one thread. (Poll: 5/9 say they have tested something here — so it half-exists already; name it.)
  • Orbit — a community-analytics bot ran in #grc-transparency in Dec 2021, then vanished. Different version: the measured "pulse" I did this morning, run monthly and automated, posted to transparency — Rick's formative feedback without anyone hand-counting.
  • The onboarding committee (a few years ago), the welcome-email automation (decayed — David sends it by hand now), the "how to use Slack" Zoom sessions (died five years ago). Different version: Tristan's onboarding group inherits all three; one session a month, one automated referrer-notify.
  • Moderators' week / Activation Team cross-meeting ("didn't work" — Governance idea table). Different version: Rick's debrief protocol run by every host + one host roundtable a quarter (Governance idea B, host development).
  • Asks that went quiet: Klaus's beta-testers (Jul 2025), Dennis's Ground Share Coops invite, Alex Popp's intros, Randy Pt2, Didi Pershouse. Different version: an Asks ledger — one thread, one owner per ask, a two-week check-in; the wins/asks vessel from Well A is the live half of this.
  • Brian's tokenized "frequent flyer" participation (Governance table, slated 2026H2). Lighter version first: recognition before tokens — anniversaries, first-time speakers, come-backs; Adam's six years is the first coin.
  • Lean Impact book club (Dec 2025 holiday slot; 3 replies). Different version: a read-and-apply sprint tied to one member's live project, not a discussion.
  • Summary sheets + Pattern-C recaps (this lane, since April). Different version: the one-paragraph signal digest — the recap people said they'd actually read.
  • Seventeen channels with a name and no pulse in a year — serious-games, integrated-learning-labs, impact-dialogue, data-hub, regeneval, network-cooperative, dao-of-dao, innerwork… Different version: an archive ceremony: close them in public, honor what they were, and move three into live formats (one serious-games session; regeneval becomes the formative-feedback home; data-hub holds the census).
  • The Permaculture Collabs funding circle (mentioned Jul 2025) and Frederick's gift table. Different version: a GRC gift round — see Enspiral's Cobudget below.
  • The Africa group runs on WhatsApp and hosts its own meetings. Not a problem to fix — a pattern to copy: lanes own their channel; bridge one way into Slack.
  • The archive itself — first governance models, comms plans, the 2021 conversations. Different version: GRC archaeology — one artifact a week, resurfaced with its author.

Well C · What neighboring networks do (sourced)

  • Enspiral (NZ) — a public handbook of working agreements; Cobudget rounds where a common pot is split equally and anyone proposes a "bucket" to fund; Loomio for async decisions. For GRC: a gift round on the Frederick model; publish Rick's facilitation handbook as the GRC handbook.
  • Presencing Institute u-lab — coaching circles of five that meet between sessions (4,600 circles, 900 prototypes), self-organized local hubs, prototypes as the unit of progress. For GRC: circles of five between calls — Rick's fishbowl and Tristan's mentoring are both versions of this.
  • Transition Network — 1,000+ local groups, 19 hubs that act as the bridge between groups and the whole; shared governance drawing on sociocracy + holacracy; explicit peer-to-peer support. For GRC: Michelle's cross-group committee is the hub function — name it that and give it the Transition playbook.
  • Deep Transformation Network (Jeremy Lent; Tally attends) — a monthly Live Network Meeting (panel → breakouts → long Q&A) and a weekly Listening Space (circle, no topic, confidential). For GRC: Open Office already is the Listening Space; the missing piece is one monthly all-network meeting where the lanes meet.
  • Catalyst 2030 — chapters by geography/language, 14 working groups, and open volunteer roles presented at gatherings. For GRC: an open-roles board — Dennis's "who's needed" made visible; poll said 2/9 "don't know how to contribute what I have."
  • Hylo (Terran Collective) — offers + requests, member directory, a home view of "what's alive," network maps of skills and flows. For GRC: the practice matters more than the tool — a standing offers/requests thread, a living directory. (David: "we need the Babel fish" — Hylo is one answer, but start with the practice.)
  • Post Growth Institute — Offers & Needs Market — a 90-minute facilitated session, tables of 6–8, "speed-dating × classifieds × time bank," run in 15+ countries. For GRC: run one in this slot — Tally's four currencies (time / skills / money / connections) as a live market. This is the most ready-made experiment on the page.

Aug 26 — the ask: not "pick from the list." Read the map and the three wells, then say what this makes you want to try — from the page or not — and which row it feeds. Each thing that gets a name + 30 days + a smallest version becomes an experiment; we want a spread across rows, not one winner. Hands already up: Tristan (onboarding), Sage (debrief; Slack-not-chat), Michelle's committee (cross-group). The backlog then goes to Governance's organizing committee as member-sourced proposals.
