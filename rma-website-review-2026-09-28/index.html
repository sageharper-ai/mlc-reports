What this is, and what we need from you

On Friday we talked about starting the website simple. This page turns what we said into a proposed plan and a first draft of each page, with real words in place. These changes haven't been built or published.

Five-minute review
  1. Open the Home page and look at H1 and H4. Does the description fit RMA, and would the right people recognize themselves? (Ma'raj asked on 9/21 what our deeper "why" is. This is a working version, not the final answer.)
  2. On the same tab, H5: may we list you? Send the name you'd like shown and one line about you.
  3. Nikki: H8 on the Home page is your project. Does the description fit, and what's its status and next step?
  4. Open the Guest page, V2: do you support the proposed recording and sharing terms?
Look at the other tabs if you have more time: the plan, how we decide, code of conduct, our week.
How to reply: answer the email this came in, with a code and one of these:
  • YES: good as is
  • NO: plus a line on why
  • EDIT: plus your wording
  • LATER: move it to a later stage
Example: "H1 EDIT: A weekly room for regenerative storytellers. H5 YES, list me as Ed Hwang, [one line]. V2 NO: clips should stay internal longer."
Suggested reply date: Wednesday, September 30.

The Regenerative Media Alliance brings together people telling stories about healthier communities and a thriving natural world. Each week we shape ideas, record conversations, and get stories ready to share. Bring your topic and get help developing it.

How a week works

Monday, we plan Wednesday's topics. Wednesday, we record together. Friday, we review the clips and decide what's ready to share.

Bring your own topic, and tell us what help you want. You might want someone with a story about it, someone who can connect it to what others are doing, or someone who'll challenge the idea.

On Wednesdays we record conversations about regenerative work to share. Please read this and agree, and you'll get the link to join us.

V2
What you're agreeing to

This session is recorded for public sharing. Clips the group approves for publishing may appear on RMA's channels and on participants' own channels.

By agreeing, you allow your image and voice to appear in those clips. If you'd rather not, this session isn't the right fit, and that's fine. Use the contact form and we'll find another way to connect.

You'll follow our code of conduct.

Code of conduct (draft)

Reviewer note: C1 and C4 draw on Friday's discussion; the rest is new wording for review. Ed offered a starter code we can compare with this.

C1Yes, and. Build on what others bring. Help each person make their point.

C2Come to co-create. Bring your project or question, and help shape other people's too.

C3Speak from your own experience. Honest disagreement is welcome. Attacks on people are not.

C4Our shared name matters. When you speak for RMA, make clear what's your own view and what the group has agreed.

C5Credit people. Share approved clips, and name the people in them.

C6Drafts stay inside. Keep clips internal until they're cleared for publishing.
